# Samudra Jaag

Samudra Jaag ("Sea Wake"), commonly shortened to "Jaag," is Dharti's dominant salty, savoury stimulant drink, brewed with seawater. It is culturally associated with shift work and port rhythm, which ties it strongly to harbour life in cities like Bandarpur.
