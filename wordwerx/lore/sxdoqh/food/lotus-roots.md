# Lotus Roots

A staple tuber crop of Songhai, grown in layered, oxygenated canal terraces with constant flow aeration. The lotus is also cultivated for its fibre. This entry merges the "Lotus" (biota) and "Lotus Roots" (resource) records into a single food entity.
