# Mama Staple

"Mama" (pronounced Mam-ah) is Dharti's carbohydrate staple: a carbohydrate-rich fungus grown in rice-grain-like shapes, harvested rapidly and re-harvested regularly. It is the easiest, cheapest, and most plentiful carbohydrate, served with most meals across all five megacities. This entry merges the earlier "Mama Fungus" (biota) and "Mama Staple" (resource) records into a single food entity. Its Dharti-wide ubiquity is exactly why a hypothetical rot or disease affecting it is repeatedly raised as a strong season-scale existential premise (see the open question).
