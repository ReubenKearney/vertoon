A staple aquatic crop of Songhai's delta agriculture, harvested in managed flowbeds — shallow flow troughs with rotational harvesting. This entry merges the "Water Chestnut Plant" (biota) and "Water Chestnuts" (resource) records into a single food entity. Its cultivation depends on the continuous-flow regime that defines Songhai.
