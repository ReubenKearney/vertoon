# Dharti Fruit Varieties

The dominant fruit spectrum on Dharti reflects its relic ecology. It includes aril fruits (jewel-like fleshy arils around seeds, slightly resinous), conifer berries (fleshy berry-like cones used fresh or as ingredients), seed fern fruits (fleshy, slightly fibrous structures harvested seasonally), and fungi-fruit hybrids (fruiting bodies used as "fruit" with a savoury or umami twist). They are harvested seasonally from native and cultivated plant and fungal sources.
