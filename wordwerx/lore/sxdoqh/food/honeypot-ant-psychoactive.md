# Honeypot Ant Psychoactive

Honeypot ants are husbanded alongside a plant containing neuroactive toxins; the ants concentrate the compound in a way that is not cost-effective to reproduce manually. It is consumed at three concentration levels — **Kosh** (fresh), **Ras** (syrup), and **Bhasma** (powder). Recreational misuse of Bhasma is outlawed. This entry merges the "Honeypot Ants" (biota) record with the psychoactive substance it produces.
