# Guild of Lamplighters

The Guild of Lamplighters is the ancient civic guild that maintains Sulawesi's lamp-based nocturnal insect management system. It is funded and administered separately from government to ensure continuity and neutrality, and funded at neighbourhood level through local collection stations or integrated collection mechanisms on each lamp.

## Norms

Lamplighters live frugally to avoid burdening communities and are officially sworn to political neutrality. Their nightly rounds require walking every street in a territory, which makes them widely known and sought out for news and advice — a clergy-adjacent role grounded in life-saving service rather than theology. The lamps are treated as insect-management infrastructure first and illumination second. Stealing from lamps is an extreme social taboo carrying severe penalties, and effectively never occurs. (These norms were previously held as a separate "Lamplighters Guild Norms" module and are consolidated here. Not to be confused with the Songhai Flowkeepers, a distinct flow-beacon order originally also called "Lamplighters.")
