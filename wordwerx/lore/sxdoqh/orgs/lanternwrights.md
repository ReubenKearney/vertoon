# Lanternwrights

The Lanternwrights are Mara's dedicated civic maintenance order, responsible for the Crown Lantern's optical cleanliness, conduit integrity, shutter discipline, and emergency changeover drills. They are the Mara analogue to Sulawesi's Guild of Lamplighters: a specialised stewardship body whose work keeps a single critical safety system — here the citywide light-and-air network — running and isolatable under fault.
