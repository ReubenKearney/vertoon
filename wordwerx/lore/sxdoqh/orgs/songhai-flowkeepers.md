# Songhai Flowkeepers

The Songhai Flowkeepers are the civic order tasked with maintaining flowlight beacons and enforcing Songhai's still-water taboos. Their roles include beacon keeper, flow inspector, and emergency dredger: they maintain flow beacons, inspect canal compliance, and respond to stagnation incidents that threaten the city's safety logic.

This entry resolves a naming collision. The source wiki contained two distinct civic orders both labelled "Lamplighters" — the Sulawesi resin-lamp Guild, and this Songhai flow-beacon order. To keep the two unambiguous, the Songhai order is renamed here to **Songhai Flowkeepers** (its roles are flow-oriented, and Bandarpur already uses a "Flowkeepers" borough name). The original "Lamplighters" label is preserved in `aka`.
