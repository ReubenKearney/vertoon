# Khanna Microfoundry Group

The Khanna Microfoundry Group is a corporate group, based in Sulawesi and led by Rajni Khanna, that is unique in partnering with and acquiring businesses across all five megacities, establishing a direct presence in each. This disrupts the long-standing Dharti norm that businesses are anchored in their home city and trade via import/export rather than direct multi-city operations. Its expansion is publicly framed as mutually beneficial but is coercive beneath the surface, including blackmail.

The Group is the season's central institutional antagonist. It views Echo as an asset to capture, control, replicate, or exploit — alive, never destroyed — and is politically allied with an expansionist figure in Mara. Indu is its embedded operative inside Team Echo. In Arc 2 its warnings about Bandarpur's tsunami and receding-water risk are materially correct, but it uses that truth to advance an expansionist agenda (humanity should spread beyond the megacities). In some notes the name has appeared as "Karna" or "Kana"; the canonical form is Khanna.
