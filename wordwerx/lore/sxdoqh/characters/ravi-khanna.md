# Ravi Khanna

Ravi is Echo's father and the builder of the Locus, and his death is the story's originating moral fracture. When Echo was born premature and close to death, Ravi took an extreme risk that effectively killed her in the process of partially digitising her brain to save her — producing the hybrid organic-and-digital consciousness that the Locus houses. He built the Locus to protect her, and was killed during the handover (the move of Echo into the Locus).

## Narrative function

Ravi's death is one of the series' foundational mysteries and emotional engines: at the time it happens the story withholds who ordered it and why the container exists, and the truth (that it traces to Rajni) is a late reveal. Indu was raised on a manipulated account of this death, taught to blame Echo — which makes Ravi's death the wound that drives Indu's infiltration and eventual turn. Earlier analysis raised an apparent paradox (Ravi built the Locus yet seemed to die opposing the work); the master canon resolves it — he digitised his own dying child to save her, built her sanctuary, and was killed during the handover, with the orderer concealed as the in-story mystery.
