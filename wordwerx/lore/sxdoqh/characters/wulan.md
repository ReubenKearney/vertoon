# Wulan

Wulan is a Sulawesi lamplighter and the team's first civic anchor, running a night crew responsible for a "light perimeter" — streets, alleys, footbridges, and the edges of habitation where the insects test defences. Their work begins before dusk, peaks in the first two hours of full dark, and becomes emergency triage until dawn. They do not just fix lamps: they decide which blocks get saved first when resources are short, which is a moral and political job. (The name appears as "Walan" in some source headers; canonical spelling is Wulan.)

## Signature skill, wound, and taboo

Their signature skill is triage and routing under panic: narrowing a failure to a single cause (fuel, wick, wind, sabotage, swarm pressure, human error), rerouting people and light to "seal the wound," and buying time, because time keeps people alive. Their core wound is that they lost someone to a preventable night failure — bureaucratically specific (a delayed requisition, a junior too proud to call for help, an inspector who signed off on substandard oil, a festival crowd that broke the perimeter geometry) — which turns every lamp into a personal debt and makes their empathy for Echo believable. Their taboo: they will not tell people they are safe if they are not, which differentiates them from politicians and creates direct conflict when Echo wants secrecy.

## Relationship to Echo and visual language

They first read Echo as a brilliant shut-in terrified of leaving the container — someone they can help, because helping is what they do — and only later grasp that the tragedy is ontological, not agoraphobic. Echo provides computation, pattern recognition, and access to hidden systems; Wulan provides ground truth, the human network, and the authority to act without explanation — neither can do the other's job. Visually: a coat or vest with reflective stitching and soot marks; a tool belt of "ritual hardware" (wick spools, oil ampoules, striker, seal stamps, chalk); a lantern that functions like a badge. In Episode 1 they rescue Neelai manually, opening a hatch Echo cannot and dousing her with a portable repellent device.
