# Mariama

Mariama is a Songhai-origin political strategist in exile, introduced in Arc 4 as a late-season stabiliser and ethical power broker rather than a cynical manipulator. She studies meaning, belief, and resilience with seriousness and restraint, and becomes a long-term stabilising intelligence within the ensemble.

## Wound and the Spiral of Becoming

Her core wound is that she once betrayed a friend for political advantage — she instrumentalised people — and she carries that moral break as guilt and restraint. She now rejects manipulative power used for self-interest, and her arc is learning to use her political skill transparently and defensively rather than suppressing her gifts or abusing them. Her contribution to the story's exploration of meaning and resilience is the **Spiral of Becoming**, presented as a practical framework for meaning, resilience, and agency without deception.

## Fate (contradiction parked)

The character was materially redefined from an earlier "spiritual/meaning architecture" concept into the present political-strategist form. Her long arc, in the documents that include it, runs from exile to a return home and an attempted benevolent leadership role — and then to an **accidental death** that reframes the story around whether change can ever safely depend on one person ("movements must outlive individuals" / "do we build systems or heroes?"). Other documents end her arc alive, with no death. This is left unresolved here rather than silently decided; see the open questions.
