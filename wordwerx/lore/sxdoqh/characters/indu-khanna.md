# Indu Khanna

Indu is a rational, forensic analyst embedded by the Khanna Microfoundry Group to infiltrate Echo's growing network and feed information to Rajni. She was raised on a manipulated account of Ravi Khanna's death and taught to despise the AI, so she begins by seeing Echo as technology to exploit. Truth is sacred to her, and living inside a lie corrodes her, which is the engine of her turn.

## Dramatic function and arc

Indu is the season's Impact Character: she first reframes Echo as a tool, then gradually becomes the force that compels recognition of Echo's personhood, and the Echo–Indu relationship moves from exploitation and distrust toward recognition and attempted protection. She is intentionally the last of the early core team to be introduced — held back until Echo has become detectable enough to attract targeted infiltration — and she enters at the Arc 1 climax through decisive competence, helping Wulan, Neelai, and Echo escape and becoming trustworthy before the later fallout. The Arc 1 post-credit beat (Indu speaking with her mother, Rajni) reveals her allegiance to the audience while the team remains unaware. Mid-series she is revealed as a Khanna-planted traitor; late-series, as Echo's sister. By season's end her journey with Echo must be established strongly enough that she tries to stop or mitigate the strike on the Locus — the culmination of moving from instrument to sister and protector, whether or not she can prevent the loss. Her public identity is as a Bandarpur analyst, separate from her true Khanna lineage.
