# Neelai

Neelai is a journalist from Mara and the audience's initial point of entry into Dharti. She is curious, expressive, emotionally intelligent, and suspicious of official narratives — characterised in the earliest character notes as an ENFP. Her narrative function is to ask the dangerous question and keep pulling hidden structure into the light, providing the conspiracy drip-feed without exposition dumps.

## Wound and taboo

Her core wound is that a previous story she published harmed someone she cared about, which drives her to balance truth with empathy rather than chase exposure for its own sake. Her taboo is that she will not silence voices.

## Role across Arc 1

In Episode 1 she investigates a lamp outpost, discovers concentration irregularities in the resin system, photographs logbook evidence, and is nearly killed when sensor protection fails. In Episode 2 she uses her real press credentials to gain access to a resin plantation and refinery, which burns her anonymity and ties her identity to the investigation permanently. Her relationship vector to Echo begins as suspicion and moves toward protectiveness; her arc runs from suspicious investigator to public truth-teller trying to weigh openness against harm.
