# Kaie

Kaie is a Xingu-origin street-urchin survivalist and infiltrator, defined by extreme mobility, survival instincts, and a resistance to permanence and attachment. They are the physical specialist for the Xingu arc and enter the story through necessity rather than invitation.

## Wound and arc

Kaie's parents were tied to suppressed knowledge about the Mother Tree's decline. Kaie begins by believing there was a murderous cover-up around their parents, then discovers a harder, sadder truth: their parents likely died in an accident caused by the tree's declining health rather than an assassination conspiracy. The emotional catharsis comes from Kaie shifting from blame to acceptance and choosing to help others face reality — moving from a rootless, attachment-averse survivor to someone willing to stay for a cause. That pivot from conspiracy fixation to ecological acceptance needs to feel earned.
