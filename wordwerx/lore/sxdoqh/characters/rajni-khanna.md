# Rajni Khanna

Rajni leads the Khanna Microfoundry Group, whose expansion across all the megacities is publicly framed as mutually beneficial but is coercive beneath the surface, including blackmail. She represents power without stewardship. She views the sentient AI as an asset to capture, control, replicate, or exploit, and is politically allied with an expansionist figure in Mara.

## Family and motive

Rajni is Echo's mother (a late-series reveal) and Indu's mother — the Arc 1 post-credit beat is Indu reporting to her. She raised Indu on a manipulated account of Ravi's death, weaponising it against Echo. Her defining constraint for plotting is that she wants Echo and the Locus technology **captured alive**, not destroyed; this is why the Season 1 strike on the Locus is framed as a subordinate or subcontracted element misinterpreting capture orders, which preserves Rajni's motive while still delivering the climactic blow.
