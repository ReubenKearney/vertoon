# Structural Batteries

Dharti's best electrical storage is materially more energy-dense than early-21st-century Earth lithium-ion — a canonical baseline of approximately three times the gravimetric and volumetric energy density. Crucially, it can be manufactured as load-bearing modules, allowing batteries to form part of a vehicle's or structure's primary load path. This is the enabling technology behind the freight fleet (the ground-effect lifter, the hybrid airship, and the hovercraft-maglev engine carriage all carry structural-battery superstructures) and is one of the few places where Dharti's power-constrained engineering buys back capability through materials cleverness rather than abundant energy.
