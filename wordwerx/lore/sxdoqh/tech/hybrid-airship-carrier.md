# Hybrid Airship Container Carrier

A hybrid airship for intercity freight, carrying a baseline 8 standard 20-foot containers (8 TEU) along established maritime corridors (route baseline Kenya to Sri Lanka). Lift is shared between a streamlined lighter-than-air envelope and aerodynamic lift generated in forward motion. Power comes from solar collection on the upper envelope plus structural-battery modules in the gondola and keel (approximately three times Earth lithium-ion energy density). It is larger than current Earth hybrid-airship concepts but within plausible envelope sizes for a dedicated freight platform; visually, a smooth pale envelope with patchwork repair panels paired with a darker, denser battery-keel superstructure and lighter alumelium framing around the cargo bays.
