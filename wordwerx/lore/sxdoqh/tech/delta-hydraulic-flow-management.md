# Delta Hydraulic Flow Management

Songhai's integrated hydraulic infrastructure: gates, pumpworks, and algae-fed turbines that regulate delta flow at the extremities, with sensor-linked sluice arrays maintaining continuous circulation and preventing stagnation. In effect Songhai operates as a living polder system — levees and raised embankments define residential edges, sluices and gated channels open during flood pulses, weirs and spillways control depth, velocity, and sediment, and gravity-fed distributary channels with low-energy pumping route floodwater away from habitation into agricultural floodplains and algae channels. The system enforces the cultural imperative that still water is taboo: every inhabited zone must maintain visible flow, and delta extremities must be actively managed to prevent stagnation and collapse. Operated by the Songhai Flowkeepers.
