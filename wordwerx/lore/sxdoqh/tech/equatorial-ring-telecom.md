# Equatorial Ring Telecom Backbone

Before satellites, Dharti's long-distance telecommunications rely on an equatorial ring of undersea and overland backbone lines, supported by maintenance waystations, cable landing sites, and high-altitude relay infrastructure. The ring topology provides redundancy by allowing data to route in either direction around the planet. It is the planetary nervous system of the pre-satellite era, later extended (and partly superseded) when Mara launches the first man-made satellites.
