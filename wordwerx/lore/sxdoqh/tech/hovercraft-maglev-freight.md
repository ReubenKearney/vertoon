# Hovercraft-Maglev Freight System

Bandarpur's mode-switching transport system, designed for an island chain with frequent landfall. On land and stable island segments the consist couples to energised maglev rails and uses electromagnetic propulsion, drawing power from the track; across open-water gaps each module rides on a hovercraft skirt, with lift and thrust from enclosed fans and ducted impellers integrated into the module body. A dedicated engine carriage houses the primary structural-battery superstructure and power electronics, freeing freight carriages for cargo. It handles up to roughly 100 km of open water between rail endpoints and recharges on the maglev network, with the core design goal being a seamless transition at shoreline terminals between hover and maglev modes without disassembly. Open-water legs are hover corridors, never across-water rail.
