# Wick Lamp Insect-Management Network

The Wick Lamp Insect-Management Network is Sulawesi's lamp-based nocturnal insect management infrastructure: resin-fuelled wick lamps arrayed throughout streets and outside homes. Their primary purpose is insect management, not illumination — they minimise light pollution and direct flame-light downward so as not to attract insects from a distance, creating localised repellence. The network is what makes Sulawesi viable as a high-density, fully-on-land megacity. The lamps are colloquially called **DALL·E** for their appearance, and are operated by the Guild of Lamplighters.

This entry also absorbs the closely related "Street Thread Censer" concept — civic insect-repellent infrastructure embedded in street lamps, using heated ceramic micro-chambers that disperse resinous repellent through lamppost vents to reduce vector swarms. The system depends on resin supply and potency, which is the failing variable at the heart of Arc 1.
