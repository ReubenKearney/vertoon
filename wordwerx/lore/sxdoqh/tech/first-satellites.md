# First Man-Made Satellites

Mara becomes the first megacity to launch man-made satellites, enabling global-scale observation and telecommunications beyond the terrestrial ring network. Orbiting platforms extend observation and communications past the relay infrastructure of the Equatorial Ring Telecom Backbone. This is a plot milestone: it is a direct product of Mara's nuclear energy advantage and a major lever in its destabilising shift in the intercity balance of power.
