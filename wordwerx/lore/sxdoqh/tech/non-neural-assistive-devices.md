# Non-Neural Assistive Devices

Cybernetic augmentation does not exist on Dharti. There are no neural-interface implants, no elective body augmentation, and no integrated human-machine prosthetics. Medical assistive devices exist, but they are mechanical or bio-ceramic and do not interface with the nervous system. This rule is load-bearing for the series: Echo's hybrid digital consciousness is a separate, bespoke anomaly and must never be treated as evidence of a broader cybernetics ecosystem. The absence of any augmentation culture is exactly what makes her existence destabilising rather than ordinary.
