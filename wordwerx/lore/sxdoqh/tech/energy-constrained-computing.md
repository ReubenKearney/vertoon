# Energy-Constrained Computing

Dharti is technologically advanced but power-constrained, with very little surplus energy. Cloud-scale computing is rare and expensive because of its energy demand, and AI is generally understood as a theoretical concept while practical progress toward advanced AI has been minimal — there is simply not enough sustainable surplus power to sustain large-scale compute. This is why Mara's nuclear transition is expected to dramatically shift global geopolitics, and why Echo matters so much: Ravi's approach enables a power-efficient form of digital consciousness in an energy-constrained civilisation, making her both a singular anomaly and a coveted prize.
