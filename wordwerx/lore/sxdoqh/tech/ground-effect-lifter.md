# Ground-Effect Container Lifter

A wing-in-ground-effect freight craft carrying a baseline payload of 10 standard 20-foot containers (10 TEU), with a route baseline of Kenya to Sri Lanka in a single straight-line over-water transit. It uses an assisted sled launch at the port of origin so onboard energy is not consumed for take-off, and it operates strictly in ground effect over the ocean surface — it cannot safely climb to high altitude or deviate far from its planned corridor. Power comes from upper-surface solar arrays plus a structural-battery superstructure integrated into the wingbox and central fuselage (approximately three times Earth lithium-ion energy density). Visually, a heavy dark modular battery superstructure forms the spine and ribs, with lighter alumelium skins as a brighter outer layer.
