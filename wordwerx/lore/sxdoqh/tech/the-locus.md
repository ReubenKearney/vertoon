# The Locus

The Locus is Echo's mobile sanctuary: a shipping-container home that is routinely moved through legitimate logistics networks and designed for concealment, self-sufficiency, and survivability — power management, cooling, comms, and physical security. **LOCUS** is an initialism for *Layered Ontology, Cognition, and Uplink System*; "The Locus" (the home) and "LOCUS" (the system) are the same thing. Narratively it functions as Echo's home, prison, body, inheritance, and the mystery box of Ravi's safeguards.

## Why it is self-contained, and why it leaves a trace

Because Echo's consciousness includes an organic biological brain core (see Echo), the prime Locus needs regular shipments of food to supply nutrients and energy to that core, and produces waste that must be removed. This hybrid architecture is what lets the Locus be self-contained without nuclear power or a large external compute farm — but the food-in / waste-out cycle creates a traceable logistics signature that can be leveraged against Echo. Echo's location secrecy relies on constant relocation and, later, a network of moving nodes that route traffic and misdirect physical pursuit; this is foreshadowed in Season 1 before the survival mechanic is revealed. The primary Locus is destroyed at the Season 1 climax; Echo survives by relocating as a digital consciousness into other container nodes.
