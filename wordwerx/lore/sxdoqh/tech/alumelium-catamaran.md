# Alumelium Catamaran Container Ship

The default ocean-freight workhorse of Dharti: a lightweight twin-hull catamaran container ship built from alumelium and bio-ceramic composites. Its propulsion mix is high-efficiency sails for primary drive plus electric auxiliary propulsion for harbour manoeuvres and emergency routing. Broad solar collection across deckhouse roofs and canopy surfaces powers operations, with batteries used primarily for port operations rather than long-haul thrust. Visually it reads as low-slung twin hulls with a wide bridge deck and dense, roof-like deck-level solar fields rather than glossy technology.
