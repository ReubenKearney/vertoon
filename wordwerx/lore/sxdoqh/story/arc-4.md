# Arc 4 — Mara, Expansion & the Strike on the Locus

This arc is Neelai returning home to Mara, where she is surprised to find the Sulawesi-based Khanna Microfoundry Group operating openly with a local leader. Mara is the great contrast city: as Mt Kenya reactivates, Mara acknowledges the need to move people out of the cliff-faced networks and uses its energy and innovation advantage to establish a new colony — the first time humanity has founded a new city in thousands of years. That proactive adaptation contrasts sharply with the fear, denial, and constrained thinking elsewhere on Dharti. Mariama is introduced here as a political strategist in exile who will become a stabiliser and ethical power broker. At the arc's end, the team and Mariama travel to Songhai, and when Echo's container arrives it is targeted and destroyed — the season cliffhanger.

## Fix to the Echo-attack plot hole

The Khanna Microfoundry Group still wants Echo alive and the Locus technology captured, not destroyed, so the strike is **not** Rajni ordering destruction. The locked fix: a subcontracted or subordinate element acts on capture orders incorrectly, causing the attack that puts Echo at risk. This preserves Rajni's core motive while still delivering a climactic strike on the sanctuary.

## Indu's season-end requirement

Before the attack, the season must establish Indu's personal journey with Echo strongly enough that she tries to stop or mitigate the strike — the culmination of moving from infiltrator and instrument to sister and protector. Even if she cannot prevent the loss, the attempt itself proves the arc is complete.
