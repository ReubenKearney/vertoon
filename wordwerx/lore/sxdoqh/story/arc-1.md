# Arc 1 — Sulawesi & the Resin Crisis

Arc 1's objective is to turn a local night-safety failure into proof that a civilisation-defining biological protection system may be degrading at source — and, in parallel, to assemble the first functioning version of Team Echo through pressure rather than roster-filling. Characters join because the case requires them. The arc stays primarily in Sulawesi to let the crisis deepen and avoid premature geographic sprawl.

## Locked spine

Episodes 1 and 2 are locked (see their entries). The entire scale and safety model of Sulawesi rests on resin potency, availability, and reliability, which has never failed in recorded history. The true cause is treated as locked at season-plan level: a **natural, irreversible biological evolution** — resin is becoming less potent over time, there is no fix, and refinement (increasing concentration) only buys time while accelerating future scarcity. The civilisation-level implication is that Sulawesi may not be permanently sustainable at its current scale, which makes neighbourhood prioritisation, public disclosure, and Wulan's triage authority morally explosive.

> Version note: the Season 1 Handoff (the most advanced source) locks the irreversible cause and the arc ending; earlier handoffs left both the cause's confirmation and the ending image open. The newer lock is followed here, with the residual choices parked above rather than silently resolved.

## Beat ladder and close

Episode 1: local system failure, first contact with Echo, Wulan's manual rescue, survival at the edge of the light perimeter. Episode 2: investigation of a resin plantation and refinery, proof of declining yield, Neelai burning her anonymity. Middle episodes: breadcrumb investigation (corporate interference, trade manipulation, plantation disease, refinery corruption, sensor-algorithm error, misread historical precedent) that preserves momentum even though the decline is ultimately natural and irreversible. **Arc climax:** a restricted-zone escape assisted by Indu, who enters through decisive competence and becomes trustworthy. The arc closes with Wulan, Neelai, and Echo escaping with Indu's help; the post-credit beat is Indu speaking with her mother, Rajni Khanna — revealing her allegiance to the audience while the team stays unaware.
