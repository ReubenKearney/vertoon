# Episode 2 — Investigation

Status: locked at act and major-beat level. Core question: if the lamp system is failing, is the resource that powers it running out? Function: move the show from survival-thriller reaction into investigation, establish Echo as strategist (not only an emergency voice), cement Neelai's decision to stay involved, and scale the stakes from one failure to systemic fragility.

## Act structure

1. **Aftermath and alignment.** At dawn, Neelai shares the photographed logbooks; Echo correlates the rising concentration values with broader historical patterns and proposes declining resin potency as the real hypothesis; Wulan stays practical and controlled; the trio choose to investigate. 2. **Access and entry.** Neelai uses her real press credentials for surface-level access to a resin plantation and refinery; the site looks orderly, but small anomalies show presentation and reality diverging. 3. **Discovery.** In a restricted section they find partially filled or empty vats, records of multi-year yield decline, and proof that increasing concentration is compensating for weaker raw resin. 4. **Exposure and escape.** They are spotted; Wulan shifts into operational mode, Echo routes them through blind spots, and the evidence is carried out under pressure. 5. **Consequence and escalation.** Outside, the meaning lands — similar data signatures may exist elsewhere, and Neelai is now attached to the breach.

## Locked outcomes

Yield decline is objectively real and documented; increased concentration is a compensatory measure, not a solution; Neelai's involvement is irreversible because her identity is tied to the investigation; the cause remains unknown, though the threat is now systemic and possibly intercity. The danger stays institutional — discovery, pursuit, exposure, reputational compromise — rather than militarised. Pointed closing question: how long before whole districts cannot be covered?
