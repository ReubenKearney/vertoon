# Season 1

Season 1 tells the story of a world discovering that its core survival assumptions are failing. It is structured as a whistle-stop tour through Dharti: a localised systems breach in Sulawesi expands outward into a planetary pattern of ecological decline, political opportunism, and escalating pressure on Echo's existence. Team Echo solves problems by uncovering truths that leave institutions, communities, and leaders less stable than before — that destabilising wake is a feature, not a flaw.

## Shape

Arc 1 (Sulawesi, resin crisis, team formation) → Arc 2 (Bandarpur, insect resilience, receding-sea/tsunami risk) → Arc 3 (Xingu, the dying Mother Tree and collective denial) → Arc 4 (Mara's expansion, then Songhai, ending in the strike on the primary Locus). Roughly six episodes per arc is the implied structure (~24 episodes), not a locked count.

## Dramatica throughlines (locked enough to guide consistency)

- **Overall Story:** civilisation trapped inside megacities facing escalating ecological, infrastructural, and geopolitical constraints before they are psychological.
- **Main Character (Echo):** the struggle between optimisation logic and emotional vulnerability; her challenge is identity, ethics, and relational risk, not physical action.
- **Impact Character (Indu):** reframes Echo as a tool, then becomes the force compelling recognition of Echo's personhood.
- **Relationship Story (Echo ↔ Indu):** from exploitation and distrust toward recognition and attempted protection.

## Signpost trajectory

1. Understanding that resin decline is systemic and unprecedented. 2. Investigating insect adaptation, Bandarpur pressure, and tsunami risk. 3. Learning the Mother Tree decline is inevitable, not sabotage. 4. Obtaining public acknowledgement of megacity instability, followed by the destruction of Echo's Locus. Echo's own signposts run from origin trauma, through awareness of the destabilising power of truth, into involuntary emotion, toward chosen vulnerability. Indu's run from "Echo as exploitable technology," to infiltrator, to sister, to protector.

## Season cliffhanger and long-arc fusion

The season ends with the destruction of the primary Locus; Echo opens the following episode disoriented but alive, before either she or the reader knows the survival mechanic (relocation into other container nodes). The late-series revelation that Echo is the biological child of Rajni and Ravi fuses family, ownership, personhood, and autonomy into the long arc.
