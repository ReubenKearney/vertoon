# Xingu Megaflora & Mother Tree

The Xingu megaflora is a single living organism: 200 hub trees — giant orange ginkgo-like canopy trees, typically 100–200 m tall, rising above the normal Amazon canopy — all branches connected to a central **Mother Tree** roughly 400 m tall and 100 m in diameter. The organism is the structural foundation that lets Xingu live above the canopy, and it is naturally repellent to Dharti's hazardous nocturnal insects, so it provides both infrastructure and insect protection at once.

For years the megaflora's growth rate and healing capacity have slowed. The Mother Tree is slowly dying of old age — not disease, mismanagement, or sabotage — and there is no recovery solution; inevitably it and all connected hubs will die and rot away. A secret core group of arborists and government officials conceal the decline while searching for options that do not exist. As the megaflora fails, Xingu loses both its structures and its insect protection, exposing it to the civilisation-wide limiting threat. Kaie's parents were tied to the suppressed truth of this decline.
