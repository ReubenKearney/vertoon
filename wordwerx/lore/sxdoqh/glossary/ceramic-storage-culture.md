# Ceramic Storage Culture

In the absence of a petrochemical era, ceramics and pottery dominate household storage and food distribution on Dharti. Markets operate with bulk receptacles, and people bring personal containers to fill and pay; single-use packaging is uncommon. Refrigeration is not widely adopted because food is plentiful and households shop frequently, relying on pantry storage in sealed pottery. Fired ceramic and pottery goods are produced at scale for storage, transport, and market distribution.
