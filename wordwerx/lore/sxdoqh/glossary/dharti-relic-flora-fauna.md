# Dharti Relic Flora & Fauna

Because the Triassic–Jurassic extinction on Dharti was not catastrophic, many ancient lineages persist. The flora is gymnosperm-dominant — cycads, relic ginkgo with non-green foliage, and horsetails. The fauna includes tuatara-cousins with a prominent parietal eye, orange crocodilian analogues, and feathered pterosaurs filling the "bird" niches. This relic ecology shapes everything from the food spectrum (aril and seed-fern fruits, conifer berries) to the visual identity of the world.
