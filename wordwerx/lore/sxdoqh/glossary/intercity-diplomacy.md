# Intercity Diplomacy

The five megacities operate as city-states without standing militaries; each maintains only a civilian police force, and intercity disputes are resolved through a mature and sophisticated diplomatic culture. The direct consequence for storytelling is that threats escalate through infrastructure failure, coercion, blackmail, scarcity, logistics, and institutional capture rather than open warfare. Two pressures now destabilise this baseline: Mara's nuclear-energy advantage and expansionism (and its culturally shocking introduction of early weapons), and the Khanna Microfoundry Group's coercive cross-city acquisition strategy, which strains the norm that businesses stay anchored to their home city.
