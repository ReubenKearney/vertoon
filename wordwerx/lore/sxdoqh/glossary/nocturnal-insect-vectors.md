# Nocturnal Insect Vectors

The hazardous nocturnal insect vectors are the single defining constraint of Dharti. The exposition foregrounds four archetypes: Anopheles mosquitoes, phlebotomine sandflies, triatomine "kissing bugs," and a dengue-style urban mosquito (Aedes-like). They make unprotected night-time lethal, and have single-handedly constrained civilisation's geographic spread, limiting humanity to five tiny, dense, hardened equatorial pockets. Every city's defining system — Sulawesi's lamps, Mara's sealed lightwells, Bandarpur's water distance and night hardening, Xingu's repellent megaflora, Songhai's sealing protocols — exists to answer this one threat.
