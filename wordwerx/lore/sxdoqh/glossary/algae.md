# Algae

A primary energy and nutrient source on Dharti's delta corridor, cultivated in flow channels. Algae-based cultivation and energy systems underpin daily life in Songhai, feeding the algae-based solar and biomass channels into which controlled floodwater is routed, and supplying the algae-fed turbines of the delta's hydraulic management. Part of the hydraulic worldbuilding consolidated under Songhai.
