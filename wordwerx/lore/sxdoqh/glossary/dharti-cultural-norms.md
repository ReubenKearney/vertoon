# Dharti Cultural Norms

The global cultural norms across Dharti's megacities emphasise practical ethics, stewardship, open knowledge, diplomacy, and restraint. Atheism is the default cultural posture, not a topic of debate: meaning and legitimacy come from practical ethics, stewardship, and civic duty rather than faith institutions. Libraries and the open dissemination of knowledge are central institutions — there are no patents or copyrights, prestige comes from contributing, combining, and applying knowledge, and a tension exists for exceptionally talented individuals whose work simply diffuses into the culture. Population is intentionally maintained just above replacement, with social pressure against large families (and tension for those who want more children) and a general reluctance to establish new settlements even where technology would allow it. Gender equality is longstanding and unremarked, attributed to long-standing food availability and the absence of historic pressures for rigid specialisation.
