# Resin Smoke

Resin smoke is both a culturally normal vice and an insect deterrent, despite its health hazards. It carries three naming registers: slang **Dhoop**, polite **Raal Dhuna**, and administrative **Aromatic Smoke**. Mechanics range from a charcoal cup and pellet for individual social consumption (non-portable, ordered while seated), to a heated stone or ceramic plate for group consumption (hookah-like), to portable options such as resin-coated fibre plugs in pipes or rolled cigarette equivalents. The same resin biology underpins the lamp fuel of the Wick Lamp Insect-Management Network — which is why the resin potency/yield decline at the centre of Arc 1 is a civilisation-level threat, not merely a cultural inconvenience.
