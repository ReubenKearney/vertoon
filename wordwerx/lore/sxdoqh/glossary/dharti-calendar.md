# Dharti Calendar

Dharti uses a single, planetwide standard timezone. People refer to local daylight terms (dawn, noon, dusk) for approximate timing and to a shared global clock for precise scheduling. This entry supersedes the placeholder 360-day "Flow Calendar."

## Year structure

The year has **10 named months**, split into two five-month seasons. Wet season (Months 1–5): Nember (Nem), Plumer (Plu), Tember (Tem), Fluver (Flu), Undmer (Und). Dry season (Months 6–10): Serenber (Ser), Arinber (Ari), Solber (Sol), Venber (Ven), Igniber (Ign). All megacities are equally affected by the Wet/Dry cycle, and the high point of each season is its third month — Tember (Wet peak) and Solber (Dry peak).

## Week and festival block

The standard **6-day week** is four work-focused days then a two-day weekend: Munday (munus, work), Tersday (tersus, work), Wirtday (virtus, work), Formday (formatio, work), Somday (somnus, rest), Laetday (laetitia, joy). The year-end festival block, **Yearturn**, sits outside the standard week and runs Mendeday, Lanternday, Vowday, and Turnday; in leap years an additional festival day, **Tidecall**, is observed.
