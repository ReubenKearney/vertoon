# Songhai

Songhai is one of Dharti's five megacities, a river-delta civilisation located along the Gambia River on the western coast of Africa. The city extends about 200 km inland from the Atlantic along the river, in an urban corridor roughly 5 km wide (2.5 km each side), surrounded by hundreds of kilometres of desert that contrast starkly with the fertile blue-green delta. Its primary power is solar. Average density is around 40,000 people per km²; built form runs roughly 80% open-trench/terraced at ~1.5 storeys, 15% up to 4 storeys, and 5% dense hubs at 12–13 storeys (no megatowers). Songhai avoids deep underground burrowing and mountain-embedded living to stay visually distinct from Mara, leaning instead into trenches, terraces, and delta layering. People also live in burrows and use cooling towers to avoid the heat and insects.

## Hydraulic culture and the still-water taboo

Songhai is defined by perpetual water flow, algae-based cultivation, and strict hydraulic management. The Gambia River is treated not as a transport thoroughfare but as a continuous public commons — marketplace, promenade, cultural spine, civic heart — with transport running parallel via extensive subways. The delta undergoes regular, controlled inundation, treated as a productive input rather than a disaster, routed away from habitation into agricultural floodplains and algae-based solar/biomass channels through an engineered polder system of levees, sluices, weirs, spillways, and gravity-fed distributary channels (see Delta Hydraulic Flow Management). Residential trench districts are hydraulically protected, never designed to flood internally. A deep cultural taboo and near-paranoia surrounds **still water** because of insect risk: stagnant water is socially unacceptable and architecturally avoided, and all water systems are designed for continuous movement. Day/night contrast is extreme — open and communal by day, sealed, hardened, and survival-focused by night, with retractable covers, shutters, meshes, and layered sealing on the trench districts. The civic order that maintains flow beacons and enforces the still-water taboo is the Songhai Flowkeepers.

## Narrative role (Arc 4 climax)

Songhai is where Season 1 ends. After Mara, the team and Mariama (a Songhai-origin political strategist in exile) travel here, and when Echo's primary Locus container arrives, it is targeted and destroyed — the season cliffhanger. The city's locked existential threat is tectonic: a shift in plates will eventually bury it.
