# Mara

Mara is a highland megacity embedded into the sides of Mt Kenya, lit and ventilated from a single summit megastructure, the Crown Lantern. Its primary power is biomass transitioning to nuclear, and that nuclear transition is the most destabilising development on Dharti: surplus energy is otherwise rare and politically meaningful, so Mara's energy advantage upends the long-standing equilibrium between cities and makes Mara a geopolitical outlier rather than simply prosperous.

## Crown Lantern

Mara's signature system is the Crown Lantern, a summit megastructure that provides citywide light distribution and hosts the primary controlled air-exchange headworks. All illumination delivered through the city's lightwell network originates at the Crown Lantern (the singular-origin rule). Full technical detail — light sourcing and conditioning, the eight sealed Light Spines, the equity rule, the circadian sky-cycle, and the safety modes — lives in the Crown Lantern Lightwell System entry. It is maintained by the Lanternwrights.

## Expansion and disruption

Mara's energy advantage enables expansionist projects that destabilise Dharti's norms. It begins establishing the first new city in centuries; it launches the first man-made satellites (global observation and telecom beyond the ring network); and it introduces early weapons and tactics that are culturally shocking in a civilisation without militaries. Mara starts as a central consolidated authority and, as it becomes expansionist, grants regional leadership to newly established cities aligned to its strategic objectives. In Arc 4, as Mt Kenya reactivates, Mara acknowledges the need to move people out of the cliff-faced networks and uses its energy and innovation advantage to found a new colony — proactive adaptation that sharply contrasts with the fear and denial elsewhere on Dharti. Neelai's home city; the Khanna Microfoundry Group is found operating openly there with a local leader.
