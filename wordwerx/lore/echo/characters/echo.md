The mysterious character around which the plot of *Echo's Location* orbits. Echo
lives off-grid inside a constantly transported shipping container — The Locus —
believing it to be a sanctuary created by her father, Ravi.

She is precise, dry, and a half-beat late when she is deciding what to withhold.
She wants to be treated as someone, not something; her flaw is that she hides her
own limits to stay useful. The slow tragedy of the series is that the group's
attempts to "help her out" rest on a mistaken model of her existence — the
problem is ontological (what she is), not circumstantial (where she is).

Her arc runs tool → accomplice → person who asks to be counted.
