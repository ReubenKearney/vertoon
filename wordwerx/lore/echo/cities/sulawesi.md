The largest city on Dharti, with a population of roughly 175 million. Sulawesi
can be planned and built as a "traditional" megacity — more akin to an Earth
megacity such as Jakarta — because the Lamplighters' lamp network makes normal
density viable despite the nocturnal insect threat. It is the only city on Dharti
that lives entirely on land.

Public insect-repellent infrastructure is delivered through extensive wick lamp
systems arrayed throughout streets and outside homes. Their primary purpose is
not illumination: they minimise light pollution and direct flame-light downward
to avoid attracting insects from a distance. The fuel is resin-based.

**Guild of Lamplighters (locked).** An ancient guild, funded and administered
separately from government to ensure continuity and neutrality. It is
neighbourhood-funded via local collection stations or collection mechanisms built
into each lamp. Stealing from lamps is an extreme social taboo with severe
penalties, effectively never occurring. Lamplighters live frugally to avoid
burdening communities and are officially sworn to political neutrality. Their
nightly rounds require walking every street in a territory, so they are widely
known and sought out for news and advice — a clergy-adjacent role grounded in
life-saving service rather than theology.
